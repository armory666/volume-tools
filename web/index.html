06 — Notes

Short answers. No brochure fog.

Who is this for?
Authorized security researchers, pentesters, and investigators. Use must stay lawful and aligned with our acceptable use rules.
How does a search work?
Your identifier fans out across configured modules and corpora. Hits are merged, ranked, and attached to your case.
Do you have an API?
Yes. Higher tiers unlock API keys and automation hooks. Exact limits depend on the plan you pick.
Can I share my account?
No. Accounts are individual. Sharing credentials violates policy and will get revoked.
Can I use this for anything?
No. Abuse, harassment, and unlawful targeting are banned. We log and act on violations.
