06 — Policies

Rules of the road.

Plain-language terms. By using the console you agree to what's below. No legalese, no surprises.

Terms of Service

Eligibility. Access is granted to a single operator per access key. Keys are personal, expire after the period shown in your console, and may not be shared, resold, or published.

Authorized use. You may use the console only for lawful investigations, fraud prevention, security research, or other purposes you are legally permitted to pursue. You are responsible for the legality of each query in your jurisdiction.

Service. Modules depend on third-party sources and may be unavailable, rate-limited, or return no record. Results are provided as-is, without warranty of accuracy or completeness.

Suspension. Accounts may be locked or removed for abuse, key sharing, evasion of rate limits, or violation of these terms.

Privacy Policy

What we store. Your username, access key, plan, credit balance, key expiry and usage counters. That's it. Queries are not logged by the server or written to the database.

Where it lives. Account records are stored in Postgres (Aiven). Sessions are HTTP-only cookies; the admin session is separate and expires after 12 hours.

What we don't do. We don't sell data, run advertising trackers, or share account records with third parties. Data-source providers receive the query only when you run a lookup.

Acceptable Use

Permitted. Fraud checks, account recovery, KYC support, threat intelligence, red-team research with authorization, and law-enforcement work.

Prohibited. Harassment, stalking, doxxing, extortion, unauthorized access to accounts, identity theft, or any use that violates applicable law. Do not use the console to target minors or to build profiles for harm.

We may refuse or revoke access where use appears abusive, without notice.

Billing & Refunds

Plans. Free includes 15 searches per day with redacted results. Paid plans add daily search credits; Lifetime is unlimited. Credits refresh every 24 hours to the plan allowance and do not accumulate.

Custom plans. Set manually by the operator with a name, credit amount, key duration and key value. DM @stomponheads on Telegram to negotiate.

Refunds. Consumed credits and expired custom periods are non-refundable. If a plan was purchased in error and no searches were used, contact the operator within 48 hours.

Data & Retention

Queries. Processed in memory and shown only in your session. Exporting results (JSON/CSV) stores the file on your machine, not on the server.

Case workspace. Cases you create are stored on the server against your account so they persist across sessions. Delete a case to remove it from the database.

Account deletion. Deleting an account removes its record and key immediately. Backups, if any, roll off within 30 days.
