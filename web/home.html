06 — Policies

Rules of the road.

Plain-language terms for using the console. By signing in you agree to the terms below.

1. Eligibility

Access is granted to a single operator per access key. Keys are personal, expire after the period shown in your console, and may not be shared, resold, or published.

2. Authorized use

You may use the console only for lawful investigations, fraud prevention, security research, or other purposes you are legally permitted to pursue. You are responsible for the legality of each query in your jurisdiction.

3. Service

Modules depend on third-party sources and may be unavailable, rate-limited, or return no record. Results are provided as-is, without warranty of accuracy or completeness.

4. Suspension

Accounts may be locked or removed for abuse, key sharing, evasion of rate limits, or violation of these terms.

What we store

Your email (if you signed in with one), access key, plan, credit balance, key expiry and usage counters. That's it. Queries are not logged by the server or written to the database unless you explicitly save them to a case.

Where it lives

Account records are stored in an Aiven Postgres database. Sessions are bearer tokens with a 30-day expiry. The admin session is separate and expires after 12 hours.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, run advertising trackers, or share account records with third parties. Data-source providers receive the query only when you run a lookup.

Permitted

Fraud checks, account recovery, KYC support, threat intelligence, red-team research with authorization, and law-enforcement work.

Prohibited

Harassment, stalking, doxxing, extortion, unauthorized access to accounts, identity theft, or any use that violates applicable law. Do not use the console to target minors or to build profiles for harm.

We may refuse or revoke access where use appears abusive, without notice.

Plans

Free includes 15 searches per day with redacted results. Paid plans add daily search credits; Lifetime is unlimited. Credits refresh every 24 hours to the plan allowance and do not accumulate.

Custom plans

Set manually by an administrator with a name, credit amount, key duration and key value. Message @stomponheads on Telegram for custom arrangements.

Refunds

Consumed credits and expired custom periods are non-refundable. If a plan was purchased in error and no searches were used, contact the administrator within 48 hours.

Query handling

Queries are processed in memory and shown only in your session. Exporting results (JSON/CSV) stores the file on your machine, not on the server.

Case workspace

When you explicitly save a hit or entity to a case, it is stored in the database so you can return to it. Deleting a case removes all its entities immediately.

Account deletion

Deleting an account removes its record and key immediately. Backups, if any, roll off within 30 days.
